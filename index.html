Our product

One place to run the whole celebration.

QCCESS

Event coordination for weddings & ceremonies

QCCESS helps planners run weddings, traditional ceremonies and the private events around them from one place. Guests register through a branded link, check in by QR code, order their aso-ebi, find their seat and pledge gifts, and the planner gets a single report at the end.

  • Guest registration
  • QR check-in
  • Aso-ebi orders
  • Seating chart
  • Gift pledges
  • One final report
